Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly: your username, email address, optional phone number, and any content you submit, including report details, images, reviews, and testimonials.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to operate the Platform: displaying your reports to other users, running our matching system to suggest potential matches, allowing other users to contact you about a specific report, sending account and password-reset emails, and improving the Platform over time.

3. What's visible to other users

Your reports (item details, location, date, image) are visible to anyone browsing the Platform. Your phone number is shown only to authenticated users viewing a specific report tied to your account, to help them verify and coordinate a return. Your email address is never shown publicly.

4. API credentials

Access tokens and API keys you generate allow you, or software you control, to access your own account data through the API. These credentials are shown to you once and are not accessible to Platform staff or other users. We are not responsible for credentials you choose to share with third parties.

5. Data retention

Your account and report data are retained as long as your account remains active. You may delete individual reports at any time. To request full account deletion, contact us via the Support page.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is only shared with other users as described in Section 3, to the extent necessary for the Platform to function.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including password hashing and secure credential storage. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through our Support page.
